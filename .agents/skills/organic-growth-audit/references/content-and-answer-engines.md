# Content and answer-engine readiness

## Map intent to helpful pages

Map query cluster → visitor question → existing URL → missing evidence → intended action. Use account/search evidence when available; otherwise label qualitative demand hypotheses. Never invent search volume, competition or positions.

For local services, relevant questions may cover location, inclusions, duration, price scope, group options, language, dietary/accessibility limits and booking. Group related questions when useful; do not generate near-identical pages for each synonym or area without real differences.

Use direct answers followed by context, limits and next steps when that helps readers. This is a writing choice, not a guaranteed retrieval tactic. Add first-hand examples, original supplied media and a named responsible expert. More words without original knowledge do not automatically improve content. Keep missing business facts in the working plan; never publish guesses.

## Trustworthy facts and relationships

Share displayed facts with JSON-LD where feasible. Use supported entity relationships, stable absolute IDs and verified profiles. Verify currency, price scope, included/excluded items, dates and edition-specific booking paths. A private quote is not a public ticket price.

Parse schema and compare visible content, then separately check current rich-result support. Useful FAQs do not require a Google FAQ feature. Markup alone is not citation evidence. Never invent aggregate ratings or convert an article publication date into an event/service date.

External references support only claims they actually verify. Distinguish publication/event dates and expertise. Do not invent a storefront, qualifications, accessibility or safety guarantees. Business listings and outreach are separate external actions requiring appropriate authorization.

## Language and geography

Give real translations their own canonical URLs, reciprocal/self language annotations where appropriate and helpful internal links. Check translated facts, links and heading structure. LocalStorage/browser preference is not a second indexable URL. Avoid forced redirects that prevent access to requested content.

For a same-URL toggle, document server default language and preserve explicit choice. Compare raw/rendered metadata and lang/dir with fresh storage, saved alternate language and explicit switching. Do not award multilingual indexing coverage for a toggle.

## Growth plan

Adapt this starting shape to actual evidence:

| Period | Outputs | Dependencies/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| First 30 days | Remove crawl barriers; confirm canonical/index status; answer missing business questions; establish query/lead baseline | Account access, actual sitemap URLs, owner-confirmed facts |
| Days 31–60 | Add original examples/media; connect related guides/translations; correct existing entity/listing facts within scope | Expert input, genuine sources, equal-window data |
| Days 61–90 | Reassess pages, queries, leads and citations; address measured gaps; useful partnerships if authorized | Recrawl evidence, comparable observations, attribution limits |

Give each action an owner, dependency, verification and success measure. Engine processing stays pending rather than triggering endless resubmission. Suggested future checks are not scheduled automations unless scheduling is requested.
